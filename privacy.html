Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Who we are

Wuy10M Analytics is an internal tool operated by Wuy10M. Contact: manhntz@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When a team member signs in with a TikTok account that we own or manage, we receive from TikTok, with that person's consent:

  • Basic profile information of the connected account: open ID, display name, avatar, follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • The list of public videos on that account and, for each video, its ID, title, description, creation time, duration, share link, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, used only to make these requests.

We do not collect data about viewers, commenters or any account that has not signed in. We do not collect passwords.

3. How we use it

The data is used solely to measure and compare the performance of our own videos. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to our own content planning.

4. Storage and security

Video statistics are stored in private files on our own computers and cloud storage accessible only to our team. Tokens are stored separately from the statistics and are never published. We keep statistics for as long as they are useful for comparing our videos; tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with any third party. The only third party involved is TikTok, which provides the data under its own Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights

Any team member can ask us to delete the data connected to their account by email. You can also revoke the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and websites; after revocation we can no longer read the account.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
