Wuy10M Analytics

Wuy10M Analytics is an internal, read-only tool used by Wuy10M to track the performance of videos published on our own TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Signs in to a TikTok account that we own, with the account owner's consent, through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Reads the public video list of that account and its view, like, comment and share counts through the TikTok Display API.
  • Stores those numbers in a private spreadsheet so we can compare how our own videos perform over time.

What it does not do

  • It does not post, edit or delete any content.
  • It does not read data of any account other than the ones that explicitly sign in.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no users outside our team.
